## Sign in

Go to [copyflock.com/login](https://copyflock.com/login). You can sign in with your password, or ask for a one-time sign-in link by email.

Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. We'll email you a link to choose a new one.

## Change your email

1. Go to **Settings** in the dashboard.
2. Under **Change email**, enter your new email and your current password.
3. Open the confirmation link we send to the new address.

Your email changes once you open the link. Until then, keep using your old address.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Settings** in the dashboard.
2. Under **Change password**, enter your current password and your new password twice.

Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

## Sign out of other devices

Under **Sessions** in **Settings**:

- **Sign out** signs you out of this browser.
- **Sign out of all devices** signs you out everywhere. Use it if you signed in on a device you no longer trust.

<Note>
  Your CopyFlock password is only for the dashboard. Your broker logins are separate. To change how CopyFlock connects to a broker account, see [Connect your accounts](/connect/tradovate).
</Note>
