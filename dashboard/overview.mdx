Sign in at [copyflock.com/dashboard](https://copyflock.com/dashboard). The menu on the left has two parts:

| Section | Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Copier** | **Overview** | Totals for all your accounts at a glance. |
| | **Accounts** | Add, change, pause and remove your lead and follower accounts. See [Managing accounts](/dashboard/managing-accounts). |
| | **Journal** | Statistics and notes for your closed trades. See [Trading journal](/dashboard/journal). |
| | **Logs** | Every copied order. See [Logs](/dashboard/logs). |
| **Account** | **Billing** | Your plan, trial and number of accounts. See [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription). |
| | **Settings** | Your email, password and sign-ins. See [Your CopyFlock account](/dashboard/account-settings). |

## The Overview page

The top of the page shows four totals:

- **Total balance**: the balance of all your connected accounts added together.
- **Total equity**: balance plus the profit or loss of open positions.
- **Open P&L**: profit or loss on positions that are open right now.
- **Accounts copying**: how many accounts are connected and copying, and how many of your plan's account seats you're using.

Below them you'll see:

- **Equity this session**: a chart of your total equity since you opened the page.
- **Recent copy activity**: the latest opens and closes copied to your followers.

If you haven't added any accounts yet, the page shows a button to add your lead account.

## Live updates

Figures update live while the page is open. If the live connection drops, the dashboard shows a notice that figures may be out of date, and it reconnects on its own.

New accounts take a minute or two to report in. Until then, their figures are empty.
