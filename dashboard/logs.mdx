The **Logs** page records every order CopyFlock copied to your followers. Use it to answer "Did that trade copy, and at what size?"

## What each entry shows

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Time** | When the copy happened. |
| **Event** | What was copied and the symbol, such as an open or a close on MNQ. |
| **Lead → follower** | The lead account the trade came from and the follower it was copied to. |
| **Status** | **Copied**, **Closed** or **Error**. |
| **Details** | A message about the copy, such as the reason for an error. Hover over it to read a long message. |

## Filter the logs

- **Account**: show all accounts, or one account.
- **Per page**: how many entries to show on each page.

Use the arrows at the bottom to move between pages.

The **Overview** page also shows your most recent copy activity.

## When something didn't copy

If a follower didn't copy a trade, or copied it at an unexpected size, check the entry's details first. Then see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

Logs appear as soon as your lead's first trade is copied. If you see **No copy activity yet**, the lead hasn't traded since your followers started.
