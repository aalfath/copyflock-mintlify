## Pricing

| Plan | Accounts | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Up to 10 | \$29 | \$290 |
| **Pro** | Up to 20 | \$49 | \$490 |
| **Unlimited** | No limit (fair use up to 200) | \$99 | \$990 |

- **Every connected account counts as one**, lead or follower. A lead plus nine followers is 10 accounts and fits Starter; a lead plus ten followers is 11 accounts and needs Pro.
- **Yearly billing gives you 2 months free** on every plan.
- **No extra fees from CopyFlock.** Some platforms charge for their own services. For example, TopstepX accounts need a ProjectX API subscription, billed by ProjectX.

Plans differ only in how many accounts you can connect. Every plan includes all features: every platform, all sizing methods, symbol mapping, account protection, the dashboard, logs, the trading journal and email support.

## The free trial

- The trial lasts **7 days** and covers **2 accounts**, enough for a lead and one follower.
- **A card is required** to start.
- **During the trial, followers copy a fixed 1 contract per trade**, whatever sizing you choose. Your sizing takes effect once your paid plan starts.
- **Cancel before the trial ends and you're not charged.** Otherwise, your subscription starts automatically and your card is charged for the plan you chose.

### Start the trial

1. [Create your account](https://copyflock.com/signup).
2. On the **Billing** page, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** and the plan you want after the trial.
3. Click **Start 7-day free trial** and enter your card details.

### End the trial early

Want your full sizing and all your accounts right away? On the **Billing** page, click **Upgrade to paid now**. You're charged for your first month or year straight away, and your plan's full number of accounts unlocks immediately.
