## Pricing

| Plan | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | \$8 per account per month |
| **Yearly** | \$80 per account per year, compared with \$96 for twelve months of monthly billing |

- **Every connected account counts as one**, lead or follower. A lead plus three followers is 4 accounts.
- **The minimum is 2 accounts**, because copying needs a lead and at least one follower.
- **The maximum is 200 accounts** per subscription.
- **No extra fees from CopyFlock.** Some platforms charge for their own services. For example, TopstepX accounts need a ProjectX API subscription, billed by ProjectX.

Every plan includes all features: every platform, all sizing methods, symbol mapping, account protection, the dashboard, logs, the trading journal and email support.

## The free trial

- The trial lasts **7 days** and covers **2 accounts**, enough for a lead and one follower.
- **A card is required** to start.
- **During the trial, followers copy a fixed 1 contract per trade**, whatever sizing you choose. Your sizing takes effect once your paid plan starts.
- **Cancel before the trial ends and you're not charged.** Otherwise, your subscription starts automatically and your card is charged for the plan you chose.

### Start the trial

1. [Create your account](https://copyflock.com/signup).
2. On the **Billing** page, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** and how many accounts you want after the trial.
3. Click **Start 7-day free trial** and enter your card details.

### End the trial early

Want your full sizing and all your accounts right away? On the **Billing** page, click **Upgrade to paid now**. You're charged for your first month or year straight away, and every account on your plan unlocks immediately.
