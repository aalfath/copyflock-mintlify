Manage everything on the **Billing** page of the dashboard. It shows your plan, its status, the number of accounts, the price and your next renewal or trial end date.

## Change the number of accounts

Each connected account uses one seat on your plan.

1. On the **Billing** page, use **+** and **−** to choose the new number of accounts.
2. Click **Add** or **Remove** and confirm.

- **Adding accounts:** you pay now for the rest of the current billing period. The confirmation shows the amount before you pay.
- **Removing accounts:** unused time is credited against your next bill.
- You can't go below 2 accounts, or below the number of accounts you have connected. To go lower, [remove accounts](/dashboard/managing-accounts#remove-an-account) on the **Accounts** page first.

During the free trial, you can change the number of accounts your plan starts with. The trial itself covers 2 accounts.

## Update your card or download invoices

On the **Billing** page, click **Manage billing**. This opens a secure billing portal where you can update your payment method and see your invoices.

## Cancel your subscription

Click **Manage billing** on the **Billing** page and cancel your plan.

- Your subscription stays active until the end of the current monthly or yearly period. Copying continues until then.
- The **Billing** page shows **Cancels at period end** and the date your access ends.
- After that, copying stops. Your accounts and settings are kept, so you can pick a plan again later and carry on.

<Note>
  Cancelling doesn't close any positions. Make sure your accounts are where you want them before your access ends.
</Note>

## If a payment fails

If a payment fails, the **Billing** page shows **Payment overdue** or **Unpaid**. Update your card through **Manage billing**. While your subscription isn't active, accounts can't copy or be changed.

## Refunds

The free trial lets you test CopyFlock before you pay, so fees are generally non-refundable, and there are no prorated refunds for unused time. We do refund:

- duplicate charges and other billing errors on our side,
- charges made after you cancelled, if you cancelled before the renewal date,
- any amount we're required to refund by law.

Refunds never cover trading losses, failed evaluations, denied payouts, or fees from brokers, prop firms or platforms.

To request a refund, email [support@copyflock.com](mailto:support@copyflock.com) from the address on your account within 30 days of the charge. Include the date and amount. Read the full [refund policy](https://copyflock.com/refund-policy).
