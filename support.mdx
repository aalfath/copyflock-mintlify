Email us at [support@copyflock.com](mailto:support@copyflock.com).

Before you write, check [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/faq). Most questions are answered there.

## What to include

To help us find the problem quickly, tell us:

- **The email on your CopyFlock account.** Write from it if you can.
- **The accounts involved**, by the names you gave them in the dashboard.
- **When it happened**, with your time zone.
- **What you expected and what happened instead.** For example: "The lead bought 2 NQ at 14:32 New York time, and the follower Apex 3 didn't copy."
- **What the [logs](/dashboard/logs) show** for that trade, if anything. A screenshot helps.

<Warning>
  Never send us your broker passwords or API keys. We'll never ask for them.
</Warning>

## Billing and refunds

For billing questions and refund requests, email [support@copyflock.com](mailto:support@copyflock.com) from the address on your account. See [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription#refunds).
