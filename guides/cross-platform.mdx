A lead and its followers don't need to be on the same platform or at the same firm. CopyFlock copies futures between Tradovate, Rithmic and TopstepX in any direction.

For example, you can trade a Tradovate account and copy it to Apex accounts on Rithmic and a Topstep account on TopstepX, all at the same time.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the lead">
    Click **Add account**, choose **Lead account** and connect it on its own platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each follower on its own platform">
    Click **Add account**, choose **Follower account**, pick the lead and connect the follower on its platform. See [Tradovate](/connect/tradovate), [Rithmic](/connect/rithmic) or [TopstepX](/connect/topstepx).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose settings per follower">
    Each follower has its own sizing, symbol mapping, limits and protection. See [Copy settings](/settings/position-size).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Place a 1-contract trade on the lead and check every follower in the [logs](/dashboard/logs).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Contract names are handled for you.** Each platform names contract months differently. CopyFlock trades the matching contract on each follower's platform. If you enter symbols yourself, in [symbol mapping](/settings/symbol-mapping) or the [symbol filter](/settings/symbol-filter), use the root such as `MNQ`.
- **Fill prices can differ.** Each follower's order is placed on its own platform after the lead fills, so prices can be slightly different.
- **Each platform has its own requirements.** TopstepX needs a ProjectX API subscription. Rithmic needs your username, password and system.
- **Each firm has its own rules.** Check every firm's rules before copying between them. See [Prop firm rules](/guides/prop-firm-rules).
