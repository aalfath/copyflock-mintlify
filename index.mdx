CopyFlock is a futures trade copier that runs in the cloud. You trade one account, the **lead**, the way you already do. CopyFlock places the same trades on all your other accounts, the **followers**, at the size you choose for each one.

Copying runs on our servers. You don't need to install anything, rent a VPS or leave your computer on. If your laptop is closed, copying keeps going.

## What you can do with CopyFlock

- **Trade many prop firm accounts at once.** Trade one evaluation or funded account and copy it to the rest, including accounts at different firms.
- **Mix platforms.** Copy between Tradovate (including NinjaTrader brokerage accounts), Rithmic and TopstepX in any direction.
- **Size each account on its own.** A \$50K evaluation and a \$150K funded account can follow the same lead at different sizes.
- **Trade micros on some accounts.** Copy the lead's NQ as MNQ, or ES as MES, on the followers you choose.
- **Protect your accounts.** Pause a follower and close its positions when its balance or equity reaches a level you set.
- **See everything in one place.** Live balance, equity and P&L for every account, a log of every copied order, and a trading journal.

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Typical accounts | How you connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tradovate](/connect/tradovate) | Tradovate and NinjaTrader brokerage accounts, prop firm accounts on Tradovate | Sign in on Tradovate's own page |
| [Rithmic](/connect/rithmic) | Apex, Bulenox, Earn2Trade, Tradeify and other prop firm accounts on Rithmic | Rithmic username, password and system |
| [TopstepX](/connect/topstepx) | Topstep Trading Combines and funded accounts | TopstepX username and a ProjectX API key |

Trade from NinjaTrader 8? CopyFlock connects to the account behind it. See [NinjaTrader accounts](/connect/ninjatrader).

<Note>
  CopyFlock only copies trades you place on your own lead account. It doesn't give trading advice or signals. Futures trading carries substantial risk. Read the [risk disclaimer](https://copyflock.com/risk-disclaimer).
</Note>
